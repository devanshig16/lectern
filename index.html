      Ready before the professor starts talking.

      No setup meeting, no hardware, no waiting two weeks for a CART writer to be scheduled.

      01

      Add your course

      Paste the syllabus or upload the slides. Lectern pulls out course terms like "nucleophile" and "Diels–Alder" so it spells them right the first time, and builds a glossary you can tap during class.

      02

      Open your laptop in class

      Tap Start. Lectern listens through your laptop mic or a clip-on mic the professor wears, and tells the professor's voice apart from questions in the room.

      03

      Read, review, study

      Captions scroll as the lecture goes. Afterward you get a searchable transcript with your bookmarks, every "this will be on the exam" moment, and a short summary.

      The lecture hall stays in the lecture hall.

      Speech recognition runs on your laptop's own chip. Classmates' questions, office-hours conversations and your transcripts never go to a server.
